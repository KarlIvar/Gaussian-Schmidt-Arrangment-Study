## Established (do not redo)

**A. The unit theorem on every slice** ([other-fields.md](../other-fields.md) Theorem 4 =
Paper II Theorem 4.2 with an arbitrary twist): for an invertible ideal \(\mathfrak{r}\) of
\(\mathcal{O}_D\) with \(\bar{\mathfrak{r}} = \mathfrak{r}\) (so \(\mathfrak{r}^2 = (N\mathfrak{r})\) automatically) and
every proper \(\mathcal{O}_D\)-ideal \(\mathfrak{b}\) — imprimitive strata via \(\mathfrak{r}\mathcal{O}'\) —
\(R_\mathfrak{b}\) is a unit, \(R_{\mathfrak{r}\mathfrak{b}} = R_\mathfrak{b}^{-1}\), \(R_{\bar{\mathfrak{b}}} = \overline{R_\mathfrak{b}}\),
\(R\) is unchanged under \(\mathfrak{r} \mapsto \mathfrak{s} = \theta\mathfrak{r}^{-1}\) and under rational
scaling of \(\mathfrak{r}\), and \(\sum_\mathfrak{b}\chi(\mathfrak{b})\log|R_\mathfrak{b}| = (1-\chi(\mathfrak{r}))(-12L'(0,\chi))\).
The odd-index theorem ([robert-index-full.md](../robert-index-full.md) Thm 3) and the
first-power Galois law ([first-power-descent.md](../first-power-descent.md) §2) are proved
by arguments that never use the Gaussian coupling \(s_0 = r_0+1\) except in Lemma 1.2 there
(uniqueness of the branch, which uses \(s_0 > r_0\)) — reuse, do not re-prove; state the
hypothesis actually needed.

**C. Imprimitive strata are carriers** (checked at three instances by exact HNF arithmetic
while writing this prompt; not in any script). At a level with \(D = f^2D'\) the stratum of

\(\mathfrak{r}_\alpha\mathcal{O}_{D'} = c\cdot\mathfrak{r}'\) with \(\mathfrak{r}'\) a primitive ambiguous ideal of

## Expected statements (derive, then prove or certify; hedge where they fail)

   \((r_0, \tfrac{b+\sqrt D}{2})\) of the primitive ambiguous forms \((r_0, 0, s_0)\), \(D = -4r_0s_0\),
