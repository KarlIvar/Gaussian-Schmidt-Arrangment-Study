## Mission

Turn "the phases are an elliptic-unit system" into a **structure theorem**, in three
steps of increasing depth:

1. **Prove that the \(\mathfrak{r}\)-twisted \(\Delta\)-ratios are units.** For a
   primitive class \(f\) of disc \(1-n^2\),
   \(R_f := u_f^6\,\beta_2^4(\beta_2-1728)^3/\bigl(\beta_1^4(\beta_1-1728)^3\bigr)\)
   satisfies \(|R_f| = g(f)/g(\mathfrak{r}_nf)\) and
   \(\sigma(R_f) = R_{f^{e(\sigma)}\mathfrak{c}(\sigma)}\); the level polynomials
   \(\prod_f(x - R_f)\) came out **palindromic integer polynomials with constant
   term 1** at \(n = 9, 11, 13, 15\) (phase-kronecker-limit Thm 5) — so the \(R_f\)
   are algebraic units there. Palindromy and constant term \(\pm1\) are already
   theorems (law 2: \(R_{\mathfrak{r}f} = 1/R_f\) exactly); **the open content is
   integrality**, i.e. \(v_\mathfrak{P}(R_f) = 0\) at every finite place.
2. **Descend to a first-power "Schmidt unit".** Find the canonical \(w_f\) with
   \(w_f^6 = R_f\) and prove its Galois/integrality properties.
3. **Compute the Robert index.** Determine (certified, ideally proved) the index of
   the subgroup generated by the phase units inside the elliptic-unit / full unit
   groups of the ring class fields — the quantitative form of the elliptic-unit
   statement, the analogue of "cyclotomic units have index \(h^+\)".

## Structural facts to use (proved or certified in the repo)

1. \(u_{\mathfrak{r}f}u_f = 1\) and the first-power descent
   \(\sigma(u_f) = u_{f^{e}\mathfrak{c}}\) are **proved**; hence
   \(R_fR_{\mathfrak{r}f} = 1\) exactly and \(\{R_f\}\) is one Galois-stable multiset
   — only integrality of \(\prod(x - R_f)\) is open.
2. \((\mu) = \mathfrak{m}_2\mathfrak{m}_1^{-1}\) (cocycle-ideal computation, moduli
   §5.5) and \(\mathfrak{r}_n\) is an **invertible** \(\mathcal{O}\)-ideal with
   \(\mathfrak{r}^2 = (r_0)\), \(N\mathfrak{r} = r_0 = \tfrac{n-1}2\).
3. The certified polynomials to reproduce and extend:
   \(x^4 - 339524x^3 - 95354x^2 - 339524x + 1\) (\(n=9\)),
   \(x^4 - 56529284x^3 + 1538876166x^2 - 56529284x + 1\) (\(n=11\)), etc.
4. **Euclidean contrast** (the consistency check for any proof): the Euclidean
   \(\Delta\)-data \(G_\mathfrak{c} = n^{12}\Delta(\Lambda_\mathfrak{c})/\Delta(\mathbb{Z}[i])\)
   are *not* units — \(D_n(0) = \pm M(n)\), nontrivial exactly at non-split primes
   (\(\Delta\)-mass law). Reading: the pair \(\Lambda_\mathfrak{c} \subset \mathbb{Z}[i]\)
   is a *vertical* chain of length = conductor in the \(p\)-isogeny volcano, while the
   hyperbolic twist \(\mathfrak{b}_1 \mapsto \mathfrak{r}^{-1}\mathfrak{b}_1\) is
   multiplication by an **invertible** ideal — a *horizontal* isogeny at every prime.
5. Gross's quasi-canonical-lifting valuation theory: \(v_p\) of \(\Delta\)-quotients
   of \(\mathcal{O}\)-lattices depends only on the position (depth) in the volcano;
   horizontal moves preserve it. (Gross, *On canonical and quasi-canonical liftings*;
   the Deuring remark after euclidean Thm 4 sketches the dictionary.)
6. Weber-function identities for the descent: \(\gamma_2 = j^{1/3}\),
   \(\gamma_3^2 = j - 1728\), and \(j' \sim \gamma_2^2\gamma_3\,\eta^4\). Hence
   $$
   w_f \;:=\; u_f\cdot
   \frac{(\gamma_2^2\gamma_3)(\tau_{\mathfrak{r}f})}{(\gamma_2^2\gamma_3)(\tau_f)}
   \qquad\text{satisfies}\qquad w_f^6 = R_f
   $$
   identically — so the first-power candidate **exists in closed form**; what needs
   proof is that this choice of sixth root is Galois-coherent (Shimura reciprocity
   for \(\gamma_2, \gamma_3\): Schertz, *Complex Multiplication*, ch. 6; watch the
   2- and 3-adic conditions on \(1-n^2\), and note \(w_f\) is an
   \(\eta^4\)-quotient times \(\varepsilon\mu^{-2}\) — exactly the Siegel-unit shape
   outlook 2.2 predicted).

## Plan

**Phase 1 — data, extended.** Recompute \(\prod_f(x - R_f)\) at 250+ digits for all
odd \(n \le 21\) (reuse `phase_klf.hyper_uf` and the certification helpers; the new
levels \(17, 19, 21\) are the real test). Certify integrality, palindromy, constant
term. Also compute the analogous objects at *imprimitive* classes and record what
breaks. Mandatory anchor: at \(n = 3\), \(h = 1\), \(R = 1\) exactly.

**Phase 2 — the unit theorem.** Prove \(v_\mathfrak{P}(R_f) = 0\) for all
\(\mathfrak{P}\): (i) away from \(r_0s_0 = \tfrac{n^2-1}4\) both lattices are locally
isomorphic and the covol-normalized \(\Delta\)-quotient is a local unit (Lang,
*Elliptic Functions* ch. 12 integrality both ways); (ii) at \(p \mid r_0s_0\), the
twist is by the invertible \(\mathfrak{r}\) — a horizontal isogeny — and Gross's
valuation formula gives equality of the two \(\Delta\)-valuations. As a corollary,
derive the **per-class valuation formula for the Euclidean \(G_\mathfrak{c}\)**
(vertical chains: valuation = quasi-canonical level sum) — this settles the
\(\lambda_n\)-law of euclidean §6.1 and upgrades the \(\Delta\)-mass law to its
per-class refinement. Verify the per-class predictions against exact data at
\(n = 7, 9, 15\) (the ladders \(7^3,7^6,7^9,7^{13}\); \(3^2,3^6,\dots\); \(5^{4k}\)
recorded in euclidean §5.5).

**Phase 3 — Schmidt units at first power.** Compute \(w_f\) numerically (250
digits, all odd \(n \le 17\)): certify \(\prod_f(x - w_f)\) — monic? integer?
palindromic with constant \(\pm1\)? one Galois orbit? Then prove the translation law
for \(w_f\) via \(\gamma_2,\gamma_3\)-reciprocity (Schertz ch. 6), handling the
\(\zeta_8\)-levels \(n^2 - 1 = 2\square\) separately. If the naive \(w_f\) fails
Galois-coherence, the certified failure pattern (which root of unity, at which
levels) is itself a result — record it and fall back to \(w^2\) or \(w^3\)
(\(\gamma_2\)-only / \(\gamma_3\)-only variants, both in closed form as in fact 6).

**Phase 4 — the Robert index.** (a) Quadratic layer, exact: for each genus character
the \(\chi\)-eigenprojection of \(\log\)-units is 1-dimensional; from the
phase-kronecker-limit tables read off the index of the projection of
\(\langle R_f\rangle\) resp. \(\langle G_\mathfrak{c}\rangle\) against
\(\log\varepsilon_{d}\) — tabulate across all computed levels and look for the
class-number pattern. (b) Cubic layer (Euclidean \(n = 9, 11, 13\)): compute the unit
group of the certified cubic subfields (LLL on the Minkowski embedding of the
certified integer cubics; fundamental-unit search with proof of fundamentality by
index bounds), then the index of the coset-product units. (c) Formulate the index
conjecture (Robert/Kubert–Lang ch. 12–13 shape: index = class number of the ring
class field up to controlled 2, 3-torsion), certify it at every computed level, and
prove whatever piece the elliptic-unit literature already covers after
normalization. Negative results are results: a non-matching index, recorded with
certified margins, still pins the theory.
